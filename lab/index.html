VIRTUAL DRUG R&D COMPANY / UNDER CONSTRUCTION

Building a self-evolving virtual drug R&D company.

Let agents collaborate, and bring algorithms and knowledge into each investigation. Self-evolution is the goal: tasks produce outputs, review and validation turn useful results into reusable skills and knowledge, and these support future tasks. Starting with peptide research, organize work around tasks with goals, constraints and acceptance criteria. A single agent can handle simple tasks; temporary teams participate as needed. Reviewed outputs feed knowledge and reusable capabilities.

This is a construction vision, not an autonomously operating drug R&D company. Existing foundations include the research platform, knowledge Wiki and engineering tools; selected checked work records appear below. Digital counterparts may be one kind of role within the organization. Computational scores, merged code and commit counts do not establish drug effectiveness.
