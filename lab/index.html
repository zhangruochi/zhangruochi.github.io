Identity · data · evidence · execution & learning

The same capability, different responsibilities.

  1. Shared contracts

    Identity, topology, units and provenance; not algorithms.

  2. Numerical providers

    Selected algorithms, models and physical engines; versions and native qualification require separate evidence.

  3. Scientific operations

    Generation, prediction, scanning, docking, refinement and simulation with explicit inputs and outputs.

  4. Composed tasks

    Compose operations; retain complete candidate pools, failures and recovery identity.

  5. Campaign strategy

    Targets, hits, H2L, leads and experimental feedback under the existing workflow controller.

Skills guide method choice and evidence boundaries. Compute, services and cognition support execution, services and learning; they are not levels of model capability. Cards use their primary responsibility; numerical providers and campaign strategies are not counted again as extra nodes.

Hit validation and hit-to-lead are distinct handoffs. Experimental feedback can return to any round. Generation, prediction and enrichment alone do not confirm binding.
