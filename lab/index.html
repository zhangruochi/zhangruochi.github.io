INTELLIGENCE FLYWHEEL / TWO LOOPS

Improve research, and how research improves.

Experimental feedback takes time and resources. I want experience to become a starting point for the next investigation sooner: one loop improves peptide design, the other improves the system producing it.

RESEARCH LOOP

Question → design → controls & evidence → revision

Outcomes and failures inform the next judgment.

⇄
HARNESS LOOP

Experience → gaps → method changes → independent evaluation

Evaluated methods return to the research loop.

This is the direction of the work. Releases still require human review; an autonomous research and merge loop has not been established by runtime evidence.

Explore how the cognitive system retains experience

Experience

Retain evidence, outcomes and failure boundaries.

Knowledge

Turn experience into sourced, retrievable knowledge.

Diagnosis

Identify method gaps and evidence-backed improvements.

Optimization

Propose versioned changes to skills, tools and workflows.

Evaluation

Independently check changes under fixed conditions; evaluate the evaluator too.
