MolFeSCue: enhancing molecular property prediction in data-limited and imbalanced contexts using few-shot and contrastive learning

Few-shot and contrastive learning for scarce and imbalanced molecular property labels.

01 / Research question

Can a molecular property predictor adapt when labels are scarce and class frequencies are uneven?

02 / Innovation

Combine a pretrained molecular graph encoder with few-shot adaptation and a dynamic contrastive objective that emphasizes difficult examples.

03 / Mechanism

A pretrained HuGIN or ChemBERTa encoder adapts through few-shot learning. Cross-entropy is combined with contrastive separation of classes; an exponential schedule gradually reduces emphasis on hard negatives.

04 / Experimental setup

Table 1 lists Tox21 8,014 compounds (9/3 train/test tasks), SIDER 1,427 (21/6), MUV 93,127 (12/5), and ToxCast 8,615 (450/167). 06 / Ablation & limitations

Figures 3–4 remove pretrained initialization, contrastive loss or its dynamic schedule. The SIDER value in Figure 3 prose differs from Table 3, so it is not combined with the main comparison. Task transfer and ROC-AUC on imbalanced data do not establish prospective screening yield.

Improvements depend on the support size and dataset; the model does not lead every 1-shot comparison.
