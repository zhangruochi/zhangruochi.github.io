RESEARCH / METHODS & CONNECTIONS

Research directions and public evidence.

Four connected directions and relationships among public papers. These are neither a fixed R&D workflow nor the contents or growth statistics of the private knowledge Wiki.

Explore four research directions

Hit Identification

I study how activity signals can point to candidates worth testing, and why similar peptide sequences can behave differently.

AMPCliff ↗

Lead Optimization

I investigate how small changes in sequence and structure affect binding, so each round of lead optimization has a basis for comparison.

PepDDG ↗

Multi-objective Optimization

I explore trade-offs among activity, selectivity, stability and developability, seeking compromises that can be explained.

Foundation Models

I study transferable representations learned from molecules and peptides, supporting prediction, screening and design while testing the limits of transfer.
