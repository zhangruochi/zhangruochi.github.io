PepLand: a large-scale pre-trained peptide representation model for a comprehensive landscape of both canonical and non-canonical amino acids

Multi-view heterogeneous graphs for peptide representations across natural and non-natural amino acids.

01 / Research question

Peptide representations need to accommodate non-canonical amino acids beyond the usual sequence alphabet.

02 / Innovation

Learn at atom and fragment scales with a heterogeneous graph, masked-fragment self-supervision and canonical-to-non-canonical pretraining.

03 / Mechanism

AdaFrag combines amino-acid fragmentation with BRICS side-chain fragments. Atom, fragment and junction relations exchange information in a heterogeneous graph. Masked pretraining first learns canonical chemistry, then adapts to noncanonical peptides.

04 / Experimental setup

The 2023 manuscript uses 7,924,509 first-stage samples and 8,977 noncanonical second-stage peptides. Downstream tasks include CPP (1,162 positives/1,162 negatives), short-sequence PROSO-II solubility and peptide affinity. ESM and small-molecule encoders are compared. Exact downstream split ratios are not stated in the inspected main text.

Author manuscript · 2023 · Tables 2, 3, 5 · Source ↗

05 / Results

Noncanonical affinity PCC reaches 0.873 after two-stage training. Canonical CPP AUC is 0.838 versus ESM 0.885. These are 2023 manuscript values, not the 2025 journal tables.

ORIGINAL FIGURE / PAPER SOURCE

How the method works.

AdaFrag adds BRICS side-chain fragments to amino-acid fragmentation, placing canonical and noncanonical chemistry in one atom/fragment heterogeneous graph. Masked learning builds representations, then two-stage pretraining adapts them to noncanonical peptides.

06 / Ablation & limitations

Tables 3–5 compare fragmentation, masking and training stages. Two stages increase noncanonical-affinity PCC from 0.827 to 0.873, but canonical-affinity PCC falls from 0.496 to 0.450. Representation quality remains task-dependent; small retrospective cases do not establish prospective candidate discovery.

Representation learning does not establish an experimentally confirmed therapeutic candidate. Generalization depends on the exact task, split and evaluation conditions.
