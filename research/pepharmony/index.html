01 / Research question

Peptide structures are flexible and complex. Sequence representations should benefit from structural information.

02 / Innovation

Align sequence and structure representations by multi-view contrastive learning; transfer structural information into the sequence encoder.

03 / Mechanism

ESM sequence and GearNet structure encoders align through contrastive training; downstream prediction keeps sequence-only input. The VAE variant additionally explores generative learning.

04 / Experimental setup

Manuscript v1 collects 17,300 PDB peptides shorter than 50 residues and AlphaFold structures; confidence-filtered AlphaFold subsets support pretraining. CPP contains 1,162 positive and 1,162 negative samples; PROSO-II supports solubility. ESM2, fine-tuned ESM and GearNet provide baselines. Exact downstream split proportions are unavailable in the inspected main text.

05 / Results

Table 1 reports CPP AUC 0.874 versus ESM2 0.864; affinity Spearman 0.447 trails fine-tuned ESM at 0.473. Final 2026 journal results are not substituted for these v1 values.

ORIGINAL METHOD / PUBLIC REPOSITORY

How the method works.

During training, an ESM sequence encoder aligns with a GearNet structure encoder. Downstream inference uses only the sequence encoder: structural information enters the representation through training.

A BOUNDED CASE / REPRESENTATIONS IN CONTEXT

Back to a peptide question.

Can structural information help a sequence representation?

CPP is a concrete test in the manuscript: add sequence–structure alignment during training, keep sequence-only downstream inputs. Same-paper CPP AUC supports a benefit on that task; the affinity counterexample shows that benefits remain task-dependent.

A public task case, not a therapeutic claim for an experimental candidate.

06 / Ablation & limitations

Table 3 CPP AUC rises from 0.843 without confidence ordering to 0.874 with ordering; diffusion initialization gives 0.855. Predicted-structure confidence and short-peptide flexibility affect representation learning. Early affinity/solubility tables contain inconsistencies, so disputed entries are excluded. Contrastive alignment does not prove uniformly superior structural understanding.

The linked author manuscript is an earlier version; the final journal citation is listed above. Representation benchmarks do not demonstrate therapeutic efficacy or deployment.
