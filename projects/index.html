COGNITIVE SYSTEM / METHODS & HARNESS EVOLUTION

Turn experience into improvements that can be evaluated.

The cognitive architecture connects experience, distillation, diagnosis and evaluation. Mechanisms do not establish effective self-evolution in operation. Candidate changes still require independent checks and authorization; the system cannot use this loop to approve its own merge.

  1. Experience

    Retain outputs, evidence, failures and applicability boundaries.

  2. Distillation

    Convert reusable experience into sourced knowledge and methods.

  3. Diagnosis

    Compare task needs with method and capability gaps.

  4. Self-model

    Record capabilities and limits to support future task selection.

  5. Candidate improvement

    Propose versioned changes to skills, tools or workflows.

  6. Fixed-condition evaluation

    Independently compare candidates with a baseline; evaluate the evaluator too.
