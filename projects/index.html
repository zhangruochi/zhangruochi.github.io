INTELLIGENCE FLYWHEEL / TWO LOOPS

Improve research, and how research improves.

Experimental feedback takes time and resources. I want experience to become a starting point for the next investigation sooner: one loop improves peptide design, the other improves the system producing it.

RESEARCH LOOP

Question → design → controls & evidence → revision

Outcomes and failures inform the next judgment.

⇄
HARNESS LOOP

Experience → gaps → method changes → independent evaluation

Evaluated methods return to the research loop.

These loops describe the goal. Changes to research capabilities still need review and validation; runtime evidence has not yet established an autonomous research and merge loop.
