What does Richard research?

Richard’s main thread is Peptide AI: learning across sequence, structure and function. PepLand learns atom/fragment representations including non-canonical peptides; PepHarmony aligns sequence and structure. PepDDG is described as binding ΔΔG prediction via information channel decomposition, with public details still pending.

What is the agentic playground doing?

It is a browser-only fictional simulation. A goal becomes a dependency-aware plan, toy evidence triggers reflection, and an isolated workflow revision is checked before adoption or rollback. A human checkpoint comes before a fictional experiment handoff. It runs no real model, training or experiment.

Where can I check the public paper evidence?

Research detail pages link final publications and verified public code where available. Scholar statistics are a user-provided screenshot snapshot dated 2026-10-08, not a live feed. A benchmark result is tied to its task and conditions; it does not establish a therapy’s efficacy.

Can you answer private company questions or contact Richard?

This preset guide answers only the approved public topics above. It cannot access private company systems or send messages.
